## How to make it

1. **Pick a theme.** A wall works best with one idea behind it: albums with covers you'd frame, the records that got you through college, or what's on repeat this month. Twelve to twenty albums fills a wall without crowding it.
2. **Create a shelf.** Sign up and name the shelf after the theme, such as "Album covers worth framing".
3. **Add your albums.** Choose Music, search an album by name and pick the right one. The cover art and artist fill in for you. For a single track or a playlist, choose Link and paste its Spotify link; it plays the same way.
4. **Put them in order.** Drag the covers around until the wall looks right. Alternating light and dark covers keeps it from looking lopsided.
5. **Write a line on each.** Add a note saying why it's there, or when to play it. The note shows below the player when someone opens the album.
6. **Publish it.** Shelves start private. Open the share menu, publish the shelf, and copy the link.

## Put the wall on your own site

Open the share menu and go to the Embed tab. Copy the auto-sizing code, and paste it into any page that accepts HTML. The wall keeps working there: visitors can click a cover, read your note and play the album without leaving your page.

If your site strips out scripts, as Squarespace and some Notion pages do, choose the fixed-height code instead.

## Already have a list?

If your albums are already in a note or a playlist description, paste the Spotify links into [Paste a list](/import). You'll see them as a shelf before you sign up.
