## How to add it

1. **Choose a focused list.** Pick books that support the role you want, rather than everything you've read. Choose ones you could happily discuss in an interview.
2. **Create a shelf.** Sign up, then give the shelf a name that says what it is, such as "Books that shaped how I lead teams".
3. **Add your books.** Search by title and pick the right match. Cover art and details are filled in for you.
4. **Add a note to the ones that matter most.** One line on what you took from a book tells a reader more than the title does.
5. **Publish it and copy the link.** Shelves start private. Open the share menu, publish the shelf, and copy its link.
6. **Add the link to your resume.** Put it somewhere it fits naturally, such as an interests or professional development section, with a clear label like "Reading list".

## If your resume is a web page

You can show the shelf itself instead of a link. Open the share menu, go to the Embed tab, and copy the snippet. Paste it into any page or site builder block that accepts HTML or an iframe.
